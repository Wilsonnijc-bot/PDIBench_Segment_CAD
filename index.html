Why the approach fails here

Two main reasons.

1

CoTracker points are unstable

Tracking fails most often during real deformation. The affected link becomes unscorable, so the deformation escapes measurement.

MISSED DEFORMATION
2

Estimation noise stacks up

SAM masks, MegaSAM depth, FoundationPose alignment, CoTracker motion, and CAD binding all add error. The final score mixes these errors with deformation.

NOISY SCORE

Next

Fix tracking before tuning the score.

1. Stabilize CoTracker

Keep points valid through real deformation.

2. Isolate each error source

Test masks, depth, pose, tracking, and CAD binding separately.

3. Calibrate last

Set per-link thresholds only after coverage is reliable.
