Does PDI measure robot deformation?

A code-to-evidence audit of the Shared Multi-Object PDI pipeline: how the edited method works, how its scores align with human labels, and why masking, tracking, and the rigidity formula fail in different ways.

197 annotated videoslinks 2–7exact-group CoTracker6 linked replays
Primary finding
The low correlation is not one bug.

Formula blindness causes real deformation to be missed. Mask and track failures create false alarms. Raw scale variance then dominates the combined PDI score.

01 / METHOD

How the edited experiment works

The edit changes a single-object benchmark into a shared-scene, per-link evaluation. Geometry is reconstructed once; segmentation, tracking, and metrics remain separate for each nominally rigid robot link.

INPUT · T × H × W

Generated robot video

LVP, COSMOS2.5, or COSMOS3 video with a Franka-style arm executing a manipulation task.

LOCALIZE · LINKS 2–7

DINOv2 + SAM3 masks

Per-link prompts and reference priors produce six time-varying masks plus a union mask for replay and background exclusion.

RECONSTRUCT · ONCE

Shared MegaSAM geometry

Depth, camera poses, and world-coordinate point maps are estimated on the full frame and reused by every link.

TRACK · EXACT-GROUP

CoTracker per link

Video features are shared, but query groups update independently so different links do not affect one another.

AUDIT · INDEPENDENTLY

Scale, trajectory, rigidity

Each link receives its own mask-height/depth, motion, and pairwise-distance audits. Cross-link rigidity pairs are prohibited.

AGGREGATE · VIDEO LEVEL

Per-link and mean PDI

Raw components are weighted 0.4 scale, 0.4 trajectory, and 0.2 rigidity. Available links are averaged for video-level analysis.

Intended design

The conceptual Shared Multi-Object PDI design.

Rigidity component

per link
rᵢⱼ(t) = dᵢⱼ(t) / dᵢⱼ(0)
ε(t) = MAD(rᵢⱼ) / median(rᵢⱼ)
score = meanₜ ε(t)

Measures disagreement among relative pair-length changes, not absolute shrinking, expansion, or topology.

Open implementation

Scale component

per link
s(t) = |log h(t) + log Z(t) − baseline|
scale = RMSEₜ s(t)
h = mask box height

Fragile under rotation, foreshortening, depth noise, and mask dropout.

Open implementation

PDI aggregation

raw components
PDI = 0.4 · scale
    + 0.4 · trajectory
    + 0.2 · rigidity

Components are not normalized to comparable variance, so nominal weights do not describe empirical influence.

03 / DIAGNOSIS

Why the score fails

“Poor tracking” is only part of the answer. The cleanest false negatives retain many tracks; the formula then discards the kind of change humans call deformation. Tracking and masking chiefly explain false positives.

Primary false-negative cause

Rigidity formula blindness

If all selected pairs shrink by the same fraction, their ratios agree. MAD is near zero, so a visibly shorter arm is scored as rigid. Median aggregation can also hide deformation affecting fewer than half of pairs; temporal means dilute brief events; occlusion copies the previous score.

0.60, 0.60, 0.60 → MAD = 0
large shortening → rigidity ≈ 0
Primary false-positive cause

Track/mask collapse becomes 1.0

Link7 masks are small, occluded, and include articulated gripper fingers. When fewer than five initial anchors or three pairs remain, the implementation returns exact 1.0 instead of unavailable.

COSMOS3 0003 · link7
0 / 100 tracks survive → rigidity = 1.0
Combined-score distortion

Unnormalized scale dominates PDI

Across LVP + COSMOS3, mean PDI correlates .996 with scale, .212 with trajectory, and −.081 with rigidity. Nominal 40/40/20 weights do not create balanced influence.

weighted SD · scale .238
trajectory .016 · rigidity .012

Attribution: formula first, tracking and masking second.

Dense-track misses such as LVP 0001 and COSMOS3 0008 prove that improving CoTracker alone cannot solve construct validity. Better masks and tracking remain essential for preventing link7 collapse and scale outliers, but the rigidity definition must change before the metric responds monotonically to shortening, swelling, and joint dislocation.

05 / NEXT

What would make the experiment valid

The repair order matters: distinguish missing evidence first, redesign rigidity second, rebalance PDI third, and only then repeat the human-label study.

01 · DATA INTEGRITY

Rerun COSMOS2.5

Use unique stems, job-private workspaces, atomic caches, and source/content validation.

02 · MISSINGNESS

Invalid, never 1.0

Insufficient anchors, pairs, visibility, masks, or depth should return NaN with explicit quality metadata.

03 · CONSTRUCT

Absolute deformation

Add rest-length or Kabsch residuals, upper-quantile aggregation, and robot kinematic constraints.

04 · VALIDATION

Monotonic tests

Inject shortening, swelling, local warp, joint separation, mask dropout, track drift, and depth noise.

After repairs, use normalized held-out component calibration, mixed-effects models, robust/rank sensitivity checks, clustered bootstrap, and multiple-testing correction.
