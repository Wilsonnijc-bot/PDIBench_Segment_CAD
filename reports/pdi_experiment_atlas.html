How to read the rigidity score

Rigidity deviation (%) = raw rigidity_component × 100. Higher values mean more deformation-like pair-distance dispersion, not a probability or confidence.

Relative bands use tertiles of the 45 primary valid whole-robot scores. They describe this benchmark cohort and are not universal deformation thresholds.

Failure diagnosis

The observed score failures come from separate mechanisms. Each needs its own validity rule rather than another global threshold.

01

Insufficient CoTracker evidence becomes 1.0

The historical rigidity function returns a maximum error when fewer than five anchors or three valid 3D pairs remain. The caller records that fallback as a successful score. Link 7 contains 34 of the 39 sentinels.

02

Link 4 loses target depth

Link 4 accounts for 51 of 59 unavailable measurements in otherwise completed videos. This makes forearm coverage asymmetric because link 5 often becomes the only surviving forearm measurement.

03

Interpolation creates scale outliers

Forty scored links use interpolation fallback, and all 40 are among the 51 combined PDI scores at or above 0.6. A missing mask produces zero pixel height, and the scale audit log-clamps that value instead of treating the frame as unavailable.

04

The CSV cannot prove CoTracker quality

The historical export contains SAM mask coverage but not requested and retained CoTracker counts. A SAM tracked fraction of 1.0 does not establish that enough point tracks survived for rigidity.

Implemented repair

Future runs now preserve insufficient evidence as missing data and export the quality fields needed for exact tracking ablations.

A

Reject insufficient tracks

A link is unavailable when fewer than five CoTracker tracks survive.

B

Raise on invalid rigidity evidence

The active multi-object path no longer converts insufficient anchors or pairs into a rigidity score of 1.0.

C

Export tracking provenance

Requested and retained track counts, retained fraction, mean visibility, and SAM mask coverage are added to future CSV exports.
