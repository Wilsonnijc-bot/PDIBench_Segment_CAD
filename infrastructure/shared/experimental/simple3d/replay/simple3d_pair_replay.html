What the colors measure

The authors’ Simple3D pipeline remains unchanged: FPFH local geometry → native MSND neighborhood scales → LFSA feature pooling → a new 5% reference coreset for this pair → nearest-prototype Euclidean feature distance → original interpolation and spatial smoothing → continuous point scores.

Red points are the selected 80, 160 or 320 largest continuous point scores. Their mean is the selected frame score; only top-80 is the official author aggregation. Top-160 and top-320 are alternative summaries of the same point scores. The video mean averages the ten selected frame scores. Red is relative anomaly evidence, with no calibrated binary deformation threshold. Heat colors share one numeric range across all currently exported pairs; the 99th percentile caps display colors only. Raw values remain on hover and in downloads.

Each test is the largest valid original mask in one of ten equal temporal bins over the later 90% of the original video. The normal image is frame 0 or the first valid image. Existing link5 masks and saved 2D track identities/visibility are reused; segmentation and tracking are not rerun. Labels do not affect selection, thresholds or scoring.

Common visibility, depth ordering, and limitations

Following the object-occlusion pipeline’s supported-pixel idea, saved tracks visible and inside link5 in both images fit one robust global 2D rotation, uniform scale and translation. Reliable track hulls prevent extrapolation. Each original-grid mask is intersected with the other’s mapped support. No point cloud, RGB, or descriptor is aligned or deformed. Weak correspondence, insufficient area, excessive depth rejection and unusable reconstructions are explicit invalid pairs.

Common masks are eroded by 3 source-image pixels by default, including at the camera boundary. A fresh pair-cropped bootstrap MegaSaM pass supplies depth for conservative 5×5 local median/MAD rejection. Only invalid depths and isolated spikes are rejected; supported depth steps are retained, and no depth or point coordinates are smoothed. Cleaned RGB/masks enter a second fresh MegaSaM pass; its depths are validated again before back-projection. Both input passes, rejected pixels and native depth arrays are retained.

Each fresh sequence uses the same eleven selected real images jointly for the native temporal tracker. The two evaluated views use pair-specific masks; the other nine views provide masked link5 context. Only the two corresponding clouds enter Simple3D. Every pair builds a new memory bank. Official per-cloud centering/unit-radius normalization and the existing voxel/uniform sampling policy remain unchanged; no 3D registration is performed.

A global image transform and tracked support estimate mutual surface visibility; they do not prove dense 3D co-visibility. Large out-of-plane changes, self-occlusion, tracking or mask errors can invalidate pairs or remain confounds. MegaSaM noise and reconstruction changes can still produce high scores. Invalid pairs never fall back to full-versus-partial comparison.
