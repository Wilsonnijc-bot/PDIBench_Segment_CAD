The source camera view projects the reconstructed scene and whole robot at the original image scale. Bright points show the selected link; 3D orbit reveals depth. V1 selects its scoring pairs at frame 0. Each pair has one reference color; orange and red show increasing deviation from the frame median. Both endpoints and their line share the color. Grey means the pair did not contribute a fresh measurement. When fewer than three pairs are visible, V1 carries the previous frame score forward; carried scores remain in the final mean. The JSON contains the exact selected track indices, original point-ID mapping, baselines, per-frame ratios, and carried-frame IDs.
