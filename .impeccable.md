## Design Context

### Users

Researchers reviewing PDI benchmark cases, scores, segmentation evidence, and replay artifacts in a local browser.

### Brand Personality

Calm, credible, quantitative. Evidence and raw values should be easy to inspect.

### Aesthetic Direction

Keep the project's existing light research-dashboard style, as requested for the selected-45 review page. Use restrained color, clear hierarchy, and compact analytical components.

### Design Principles

- Put source evidence and comparisons close to the scores they explain.
- Label mask and scoring variants precisely, including missing results.
- Keep long case collections scannable with disclosure controls.
- Preserve direct links to the underlying metrics and replay artifacts.
